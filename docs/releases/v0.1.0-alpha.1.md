## The first alpha

Pokémon Platinum running natively on the 3DS, built from your own cartridge.
It's an **alpha**: playable, but with plenty of bugs. Keep a backup of your
save, and report what you find on [GitHub](https://github.com/GabeSud0/pokeplatinum-3ds/issues)
or [Discord](https://discord.gg/YjyA5ygpC), with the log from
`sd:/3ds/pokeplatinum/log.txt`.

### What's in it

- Both screens filled on a New 3DS: the upper screen's menus, text boxes and
  battles across its full width; never stretched.
- A menu on the bottom screen: the Town Map, Pokémon, bag, trainer card,
  Pokédex, Pokétch, save and options.
- 60 frames a second in the field, and smoother 3D.
- The HOME Menu version, with an animated banner made from your cartridge.

### Known issues

- Eterna Forest: the framerate drops, the clouds are stretched, and the
  partner who follows you jumps around.
- The Pokédex's "registered" text box isn't one even rectangle.
- The learn-a-move screen in the bottom menu isn't finished.
