## The second alpha

A big update to the first alpha: a Pokémon that follows you, the overworld at
the DS's own pixel size, the wider view filled in past the maps' edges, and a
steadier 60 frames a second almost everywhere. It's still an **alpha**: keep a
backup of your save, and report what you find on
[GitHub](https://github.com/GabeSud0/pokeplatinum-3ds/issues) or
[Discord](https://discord.gg/YjyA5ygpC), with the log from
`sd:/3ds/pokeplatinum/log.txt`.

### Updating from the first alpha

For this update, replace **all three files**. Build them again on the
website from your cartridge, then:

1. **The data pack (`platinum3ds.pak`):** copy the new one over the old one.
   It has the follower Pokémon's sprites and Poké Ball, which the first
   alpha's pack doesn't.
2. **The game (`pokeplatinum.3dsx`):** copy the new one over the old one.
3. **The HOME Menu version (`.cia`):** install the new one with FBI, over
   the old one. It now opens the `.3dsx`, so after this update you'll only
   need to replace the `.3dsx` (and the `.pak` when a release says so).

Your save isn't touched, but keep a backup of it anyway.

### New

- **A Pokémon that follows you:** turn on FOLLOWER in the enhancements, and
  the first Pokémon in your party walks behind you, with its own Poké Ball.
- **PIXEL PERFECT:** the overworld at the DS's own pixel size across the
  whole upper screen, with the people moving smoothly over the ground.
- **The wider view filled in:** past the edges of the maps, the trees, cliffs,
  rivers and coasts carry on instead of black or mirrored areas, on most
  routes and towns (still a work in progress).
- HM quality of life improvements and smoother animations.
- Smoother 60 FPS and performance.
- The Underground works.

### Fixed

- Weather effects and broken parts of the map.
- The Pokédex's "registered" text box.
- The egg hatching bug.
- People who walk off the screen in cutscenes disappearing. This will keep
  improving.
- Trees you cut came back as soon as you got slightly far away.
- People at the sides of the wider screen appear on time, on the ground.
- The starter Poké Balls in Rowan's briefcase, the duplicate people on
  Route 204, Valley Windworks' river, Veilstone's black square.

### Known issues

- The trainer battle intro's Poké Ball: some of its pixels turn see-through
  for a moment.
- Eterna Forest: the patches of light grow bigger in the last frames of a
  battle start; in a dark cave the Flash circle does too.
- Twinleaf at night: the shadows can shake.
- A short hiccup on Route 208, and sometimes a freeze of about a second on
  entering a town (the game carries on).
