## The second alpha

A big update to the first alpha: a Pokémon that follows you, the overworld at
the DS's own pixel size, the wider view filled in past the maps' edges, and a
steadier 60 frames a second almost everywhere. It's still an **alpha**: keep a
backup of your save, and report what you find on
[GitHub](https://github.com/GabeSud0/pokeplatinum-3ds/issues) or
[Discord](https://discord.gg/YjyA5ygpC), with the log from
`sd:/3ds/pokeplatinum/log.txt`.

### Updating

- Copy the new `pokeplatinum.3dsx` over the old one.
- **Make the data pack again** on the website if you want the follower
  Pokémon with its own sprites and Poké Ball. With the first alpha's pack the
  game still works, but the follower falls back to Platinum's own sprites.
- **Install the HOME Menu version (.cia) once more**: it now opens the
  `.3dsx`, so after this you only ever need to replace the `.3dsx`.

### New

- **A Pokémon that follows you:** turn on FOLLOWER in the enhancements, and
  the first Pokémon in your party walks behind you, with its own Poké Ball.
  It follows you down ledges, through doors, up stairs and into caves, goes
  into its ball for cutscenes, and comes back out after battles.
- **PIXEL PERFECT:** the overworld at the DS's own pixel size across the
  whole upper screen, with the people moving smoothly over the ground.
- **The wider view filled in:** past the edges of the maps, the trees, cliffs,
  rivers and coasts carry on instead of black or mirrored areas, on most
  routes and towns.
- **The HOME Menu version** opens the game from the HOME Menu as its own
  title, with a new icon and the 3DS's start-up logo.
- Rock Smash and Rock Climb ask only the first time on each map.
- The version is shown on the title screen.
- A bug report form on GitHub.

### Smoother

- A steadier 60 frames a second in towns, at the crossings between routes,
  on the Cycling Road, in Eterna Forest, in Amity Square, in the rain, and in
  the HM animation.
- The wild battle start in grass, the place name signs, and the fades in and
  out of buildings and forests at 60.
- Fewer stutters loading the next part of a map.
- The speed-up (4x) holds its speed.

### Fixed

- Eterna Forest: the clouds aren't stretched, and Cheryl walks steadily.
- The Pokédex's "registered" text box is one even rectangle.
- The Underground works.
- Battle HP and EXP bars move smoothly.
- Egg hatching, YES/NO boxes and long lines of text on the wider screen.
- People who walk off the screen in cutscenes (Cyrus, Roark, Rowan and Dawn,
  Looker and others) walk all the way out of sight.
- Trees you cut no longer come back when you look at them from the next map.
- People at the sides of the wider screen appear on time, on the ground.
- The starter Poké Balls in Rowan's briefcase, the duplicate people on
  Route 204, Valley Windworks' river, Veilstone's black square.

### Known issues

- The trainer battle intro's Poké Ball: some of its pixels turn see-through
  for a moment.
- Eterna Forest: the patches of light grow bigger in the last frames of a
  battle start; in a dark cave the Flash circle does too.
- Twinleaf at night: the shadows can shake.
- A short hiccup on Route 208, and sometimes a freeze of about a second on
  entering a town (the game carries on).
- The learn-a-move screen in the bottom menu isn't finished.
